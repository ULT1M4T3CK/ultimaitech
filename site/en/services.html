MKB Digital Flow

Intake & process mapping → pilot → review → phased rollout

The MKB Digital Flow is my four-step method for SMEs — MKB is the Dutch term for small and mid-sized business. Four steps until people book themselves. First we check whether your current site can take a layer.

  1. 1. Intake & stack review

    We map processes, people and existing tools. If a smaller fix or standard SaaS is enough, I say so.

  2. 2. Quick-win pilot

    Smallest useful step: a booking layer or a website that books. Fixed price, clear scope, real tasks — not demo theatre.

  3. 3. Evaluation & ROI check

    Does it work in practice? Less manual work, fewer errors, faster follow-up? Then we choose the next phase.

  4. 4. Phased rollout

    Extend in modules. Training, documentation and handover belong with every delivery — so you are not locked in.
