Definition

What is a website with a booking system?

A website with a booking system combines a professional business website with an online reservation tool that lets customers book appointments or services directly — linked straight to your calendar. ultimAItech builds these websites to order for entrepreneurs in Drenthe and Overijssel, with calendar integration, optional payment processing, and automatic confirmations.

A booking system significantly reduces no-show rates (customers who pay upfront turn up), saves you time on the phone, and makes your business reachable for new appointments around the clock. Fixed price €1,500 or €3,500.
