# An AI chatbot on your website: what does it deliver for SMBs?

AI chatbots are everywhere. Large companies have used them for years for customer service, but the technology is now accessible to every business — even if you're a sole proprietor or a small team.

But is it worth the investment? In this article, I explain what an AI chatbot does, when it makes sense, and when you're better off skipping it.

## What does an AI chatbot actually do?

A modern AI chatbot isn't a scripted menu with predetermined answers. It's a conversational interface powered by a language model (like GPT or Claude) and trained on your own content.

In practice, that means:
- A visitor asks a question in their own words
- The chatbot searches for the answer in your website content
- The answer is formulated in natural language
- If the answer isn't available, the bot refers to contact

The difference from chatbots five years ago is enormous. Current AI chatbots understand context, can ask follow-up questions, and give answers that are actually useful.

## When is a chatbot worthwhile?

### Yes, if:

**You keep answering the same questions.** Think opening hours, prices, delivery terms, service area. If you answer the same emails every week, a chatbot can take that over.

**You have visitors outside office hours.** A recreation business, a webshop, a restaurant — your website runs 24/7, but you're not always there. A chatbot catches visitors when you're unavailable.

**You have a lot of information on your site.** The more content, the harder it is for visitors to find what they're looking for. A chatbot acts as a smart search function.

**You want to capture leads.** The chatbot answers the question and casually asks for contact details. That creates touchpoints you'd otherwise miss.

### No, if:

**Your website has little content.** A chatbot is only as good as the knowledge you give it. With three pages of content, there isn't enough to train on.

**Personal contact is your USP.** If customers come to you precisely because they want to speak to a person, a chatbot can detract from that experience.

**Your budget is very limited.** A good chatbot requires initial setup and ongoing hosting. If every euro counts, invest first in a fast, findable website.

## Practical example: boat rental

Say you run a boat rental business. Visitors want to know:
- Which boats are available and what they cost
- Whether they need a boating license
- What the opening hours are
- Whether they can sail to Giethoorn (a popular village in Overijssel) from your location
- Whether pets are allowed

All this information is on your website, but visitors can't find it — or don't want to search. A chatbot answers within 5 seconds. The visitor is helped, and you don't have to type the same email for the fifth time that day.

## What to look for when choosing a chatbot?

### 1. Trained on your content
The chatbot must answer based on what's on your website — not based on general internet knowledge. Otherwise it gives wrong information.

### 2. Customizable appearance
The chatbot should match your brand. Color, position, welcome message, language — everything adjustable.

### 3. Privacy and GDPR
Are conversations stored? Where are the servers? Is personal data shared with third parties? This must be clear and GDPR-compliant.

### 4. Fallback to human contact
If the chatbot doesn't know the answer, it should refer the visitor — not give a wrong answer. A good fallback is crucial for trust.

### 5. Conversation insights
You want to see which questions visitors ask. That gives valuable insights: what information are people missing on your site? Where do they drop off?

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the chatbot take orders?**
Not directly. A chatbot answers questions and captures leads. For orders, you refer to your webshop or booking system.

**Does it work in multiple languages?**
Yes. Modern AI chatbots automatically switch to the visitor's language — Dutch, English, German, and more.

**Can I turn off the chatbot if I don't like it?**
Yes. It's a script on your website that you can remove at any time.

**How long does it take to set up a chatbot?**
A working chatbot can be live within 1–3 business days. Training and fine-tuning take another 1–2 weeks.

## Summary

- An AI chatbot answers visitor questions 24/7 based on your content
- Worthwhile if you get many repeated questions or have visitors outside office hours
- Cost: €500–€2,000 one-time, €50–€150/month ongoing
- Watch for GDPR, fallback, and training on your own content
- Only start if you have enough content on your site
