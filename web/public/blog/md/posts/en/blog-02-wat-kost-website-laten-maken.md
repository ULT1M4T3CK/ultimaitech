# What does it cost to get a website built in 2026?

"What does a website cost?" is the most common question I get. The honest answer: it depends. But that's not a useful answer when you need to set a budget.

In this article, I give concrete price ranges, explain what drives the price, and help you estimate which type of website fits your situation.

## The short version

| Type of website | Price range | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| DIY (Wix, Squarespace) | €0 – €500 | 1–4 weeks |
| Template website (WordPress theme) | €500 – €2,000 | 1–3 weeks |
| Professional brochure site | €1,500 – €4,000 | 2–4 weeks |
| Custom business website | €3,000 – €8,000 | 4–8 weeks |
| Webshop | €4,000 – €15,000 | 6–12 weeks |
| Custom web application | €8,000 – €30,000+ | 8–20 weeks |

These prices include design, build, and basic optimization. Hosting, maintenance, and content (text, photos) are usually separate.

## What drives the price?

### 1. Complexity

A website with five fixed pages is fundamentally different from a webshop with 500 products, a search function, and a connection to your accounting software. More functionality = more hours = higher price.

Examples of things that increase the price:
- Multilingual support (NL/EN/DE)
- Product catalog with filters and search
- Integrations with external systems (accounting, CRM, inventory)
- User accounts and login functionality
- Custom forms or configurators

### 2. Design

Are you working with an existing template or is everything designed from scratch? Custom design costs more, but delivers a unique look that fits your brand — no cookie-cutter feel.

The investment in design pays off: visitors largely judge your company's reliability based on how your website looks.

### 3. Type of provider

| Provider | Hourly rate (indicative) | Strong in |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer (starter) | €50 – €75 | Small projects, personal contact |
| Freelancer (experienced) | €75 – €110 | Custom work, specific expertise |
| Small agency (2–5 people) | €85 – €125 | Design + development combined |
| Mid-size agency | €100 – €150 | Complex projects, multiple disciplines |

In Drenthe and Overijssel, rates are on average 10–20% lower than in the Randstad, at comparable quality.

### 4. Technology

WordPress is the most popular platform and often the cheapest option. But cheap isn't always advantageous:

- **WordPress + theme:** lowest entry price, but dependent on plugins that break during updates
- **WordPress + custom theme:** better quality, higher price
- **Custom (React, Next.js, etc.):** highest price, but fastest sites and most flexibility
- **Webshop platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce):** separate pricing model with monthly costs

## Hidden costs to watch for

### Hosting
€5 – €50 per month, depending on the type. Cheap hosting is slow and unreliable. Budget €10–€25/month for decent hosting.

### SSL certificate
Nowadays free via Let's Encrypt at most hosting providers. If someone charges you for this, look elsewhere.

### Maintenance
WordPress sites need regular updates (core, theme, plugins). Budget €50–€150/month for a maintenance package, or do it yourself if you're technically inclined.

### Content
Writing text, taking or commissioning photos, video — this is often underestimated. Budget separately: €500–€2,000 for a complete set of business copy from a copywriter.

### Domain name
€10–€15 per year for a .nl domain. Make sure the domain is registered in your name, not the web developer's.

## When is cheap too cheap?

A €300 website sounds attractive, but consider what you get:
- A template thousands of other sites also use
- No customization, no own identity
- Limited SEO optimization
- Unclear aftercare

Your website is often the first contact potential customers have with your business. If that first impression isn't professional, you lose the customer before you get a chance to tell your story.

## Save smart without sacrificing quality

- **Provide content yourself.** Write your own text and take your own photos. That easily saves €500–€1,500.
- **Start small, build out.** Begin with a solid base of 5–8 pages and add functionality later.
- **Choose fixed price.** No surprises afterward. You know exactly what you're paying.
- **Invest in speed and SEO.** A fast, findable site pays for itself.

## Summary

- A professional business website in 2026 costs between €2,000 and €8,000
- Complexity, design, and type of provider determine the price
- Watch for hidden costs: hosting, maintenance, content
- Don't choose the cheapest option — choose the best balance between price and quality
- Fixed-price agreements give certainty
